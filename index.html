ABOUT US

GEPCO Training and Development Centre (GTDC)

GTDC is committed to enhancing the skills, knowledge and professional competencies of its workforce. We provide structured training programs, practical sessions, development opportunities to build a safer, more efficient and future-ready team.

We focus on modern skills, hands-on experience and professional development so our students can confidently enter into the professional world.
